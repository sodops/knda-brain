## Task 1 -  analitikadan iborat unda 

- **TREND** 
- **COMPARISON**
- **MAP**
- **PROGRESS** 

Tipidagi malumotlar bo'ladi ularga mavzudan kelib chiqqan holda o'zining yozilish qoidalari bo'yicha ma'lumotlar yoziladi.
Bu taskda sinonimlarni bilish juda foydali , chunki bu taskda shunaqa paraphrasing skillar  muhim turadi, va keyin aniq o'zgaruvchilarni aniqlash kerak, eng baland va past qiymatlarni solishtirish muhim

Undan tashaqari Writing skillari ning o'zining baholash tizimi bor, Ular:

1. **Achievement (25%):** Savolga to'liq javob berdingizmi? Overview bormi?
    
2. **Coherence and Cohesion (25%):** Matn mantiqiymi? Bog'lovchi so'zlar to'g'ri ishlatilganmi?
    
3. **Lexical Resource (25%):** So'z boyligingiz qanchalik keng? (Paraphrase qila oldingizmi?)
    
4. **Grammatical Range and Accuracy (25%):** Grammatik xatolar bormi? Gaplar turli xilmi?





### TASK 1 — Grafik/diagramma shablon

```
INTRO (1 jumla — topshiriqni paraphrase qil):
The [chart/graph/table] illustrates [nima haqida] 
over the period of [vaqt].

OVERVIEW (2 jumla — eng muhim trend):
Overall, it is clear that [eng katta o'zgarish].
While [A] showed [trend], [B] experienced [trend].

BODY 1 — Birinchi guruh/kategoriya:
In [yil], [subject] stood at [raqam], 
before [rising/falling] to [raqam] by [yil].

BODY 2 — Ikkinchi guruh/kategoriya:
In contrast, [subject] began at [raqam] 
and [gradually/sharply] [increased/decreased] 
to reach [raqam] in [yil].
```


### TASK 2 — Essay shablon

```
INTRO (2 jumla):
[Topic ni o'z so'zing bilan ayt]. 
While some argue that [qarshi fikr], 
I strongly believe that [sening fikring].

BODY 1 — Asosiy argument (3-4 jumla):
The primary reason for this is that [sabab].
For instance, [real misol].
This clearly demonstrates that [xulosa].

BODY 2 — Ikkinchi argument (3-4 jumla):
Furthermore, [ikkinchi sabab].
A clear example of this can be seen in [misol].
Therefore, it is evident that [xulosa].

CONCLUSION (2 jumla):
In conclusion, although [qarshi fikrni tan ol],
I maintain that [sening fikring takror].
```
