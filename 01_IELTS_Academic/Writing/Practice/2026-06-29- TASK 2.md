Some people say that the use of technologies for communication can negatively affect children's literacy skills .I strongly believe that the use of mobile phones or computer  in daily life does not a pose a threat. When utilized correctly this tools can significantly enhance their language development

The primary reasons for this is that computers and smartphones grant children instant access to digital libraries .Furthermore, if child easily lose interest on traditional education, gamified apps can stimulate their engagement and help them learn hard grammatical rules and new word by playing.

On the other hand , using technologies to much can have some bad effect on how children read and write. Especially, many young people use emojis when they talk online with friends. Because they get used to this shortcut language, they often forget how to  write full sentences or spell the words in school.Additionally, because of modern social media platforms, the attention span of children has decreased, and they do not want to read long books anymore.

in conclusion , using technology for communication has both good and bad sides.I believe that it is very helpful. If they use it correctly.
