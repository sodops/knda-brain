**Many criminals commit further crimes as soon as they are released from prison.**
**What do you think are the causes for this?**
**what possible solutions can you suggest?**


It is often seen that a significant number of former inmates return to a life of crime shortly after the release.This essay will argue that the lack of employment  opportunities  and social stigma are the primary cause of this trend , and propose that vocational training and psychological support  are essential solutions.

The primary reason for re-offending is the difficulty of finding a job. Most companies are often reluctant to hire  people with a criminal record, which means ex-prisoners  have no legal way to earn money. Moreover,  the social stigma associated with having been in prison often leads to isolation.When former inmates  are rejected by their families and communities, the may feel that returning to a life of crime is their only way to survive.
To address this issue, several measures can be taken. Firstly, the government must provide vocational training courses such as carpentry,craftsmanship and computer literacy while inmates still in prison.This action ensures that they have marketable skill to find legal work after their release. Secondly ,  psychological support and counseling should be offered to help former prisoners rebuild their self-confidence and change their mindset towards society.The combination of steps can reduce the rates of recidivism.
In conclusion, although the lack of jobs and social rejection lead many to commit crimes again. I believe that providing education and mental  support can solve this problem
