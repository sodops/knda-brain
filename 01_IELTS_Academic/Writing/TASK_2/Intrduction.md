IELTS Writing Task 2 da inshoni (essay) boshlash, ya'ni **Introduction (Kirish)** qismini yozish uchun eng samarali va imtihon oluvchilar (examiners) tomonidan kutiladigan eng yaxshi taktika **2 ta gapdan iborat formula** hisoblanadi.

Ko'p vaqt yo'qotmaslik va fikrni chalg'itmaslik uchun kirish qismiga **5 daqiqadan ko'p vaqt sarflamaslik** kerak. Quyida buni qanday mukammal qilish siri keltirilgan:

## 2 Qadamli Kirish Formulasi

### 1-qadam: Paraphrase (Mavzuni qayta ifodalash)

Berilgan topshiriq (prompt) matnini o'z so'zlaringiz bilan, ma'nosini o'zgartirmasdan qayta yozasiz. Buning uchun sinonimlar va grammatik strukturani o'zgartirish (masalan, active -> passive) usulidan foydalaniladi. Hech qachon savolni aynan ko'chirmang, aks holda bu so'zlar hisobga olinmaydi.

### 2-qadam: Thesis Statement (Inshoning asosiy maqsadi/Sizning fikringiz)

Bu kirishning eng muhim gapi. Bu erda siz:

- Berilgan savolga to'g'ridan-to'g'ri javob berasiz (rozisiz yoki qarshi, afzalligi ko'pmi yoki kamchiligi).
    
- Inshoning keyingi paragraflarida nima haqida gapirishingizni qisqacha ko'rsatib ketasiz.
    

## Amaliy Misol Ustida Ko'ramiz

> **Mavzu (Topic):** _Some people think that universities should provide graduates with the knowledge and skills needed in the workplace. Others think that the true function of a university should be to give access to knowledge for its own sake, regardless of whether the course is useful to an employer._

Ushbu mavzu uchun kirish qismini quyidagicha boshlash mumkin:

|**Qadam**|**Qanday yoziladi?**|**Misol (Ingliz tilida)**|
|---|---|---|
|**1. Paraphrase**|_It is often argued that..._ yoki _While some people believe that..._ kabi iboralar bilan boshlanadi.|"While some people believe that the primary role of higher education is to prepare students for their future careers, others argue that academic institutions should focus purely on providing knowledge."|
|**2. Thesis Statement**|_In my opinion, ..._ yoki _This essay will discuss..._ orqali o'z pozitsiyangizni berasiz.|"In my opinion, universities should strike a balance between practical job training and theoretical education to ensure students' well-rounded development."|

## Kirish Qismini Tez va Oson Boshlash Uchun Shablonlar (Templates)

Mavzu turiga qarab quyidagi tayyor konstruksiyalardan foydalansangiz, imtihonda vaqtni ancha tejaysiz:

### 1. Opinion / Agree or Disagree Essays (Fikr bildirish)

- "It is often said that **[Mavzu]**. I completely agree with this view because **[1-sabab]** and **[2-sabab]**."
    
- "While there is a growing opinion that **[Mavzu]**, I personally believe that **[Sizning fikringiz]**."
    

### 2. Discussion (Ikki tomonlama fikrni muhokama qilish)

- "People hold different views about whether **[1-fikr]** or **[2-fikr]**. This essay will discuss both sides of the argument and explain why I believe **[Sizning fikringiz]**."
    

### 3. Problem and Solution (Muammo va yechim)

- "In recent years, **[Muammo]** has become a major concern in many societies. This essay will analyze the main causes of this issue and propose some practical solutions."
    

> 💡 **Oltin qoida:** Kirish qismida juda uzun yoki murakkab "background" (umumiy ma'lumot) berishga urinmang. Qanchalik aniq, lo'nda (clear and concise) va savolga to'g'ridan-to'g'ri yo'naltirilgan bo'lsa, **Coherence and Cohesion** (mantiqiy bog'liqlik) mezoni uchun shunchalik yuqori ball olasiz.
