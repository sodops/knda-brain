### 📈 1. O‘sish (Increase) uchun:

- **Rise / Rose:** Ko‘tarilmoq
    
- **Increase / Increased:** Oshmoq
    
- **Grow / Grew:** O‘smoq
    
- **Climb / Climbed:** Ko‘tarilmoq (ko‘pincha sekin o‘sish uchun)
    
- **Peak / Peaked at:** Cho‘qqisiga chiqmoq (eng baland nuqta uchun)
    

### 📉 2. Pasayish (Decrease) uchun:

- **Fall / Fell:** Tushmoq
    
- **Decrease / Decreased:** Kamaymoq
    
- **Decline / Declined:** Pasaymoq
    
- **Drop / Dropped:** Keskin tushmoq
    
- **Hit a low of:** Eng past nuqtasiga tushmoq
    

### ⚡ 3. O‘zgarishning "darajasi" (Sifatlar):

Faqat "oshdi" deyish kamlik qiladi. Qanchalik oshganini ham aytish kerak:

- **Significantly / Dramatically:** Juda katta darajada (katta sakrash bo‘lsa)
    
- **Steadily:** Barqaror ravishda (bir maromda o‘ssa)
    
- **Slightly / Gradually:** Biroz, sekin-asta
