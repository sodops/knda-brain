The video is a comprehensive guide to all essential vitamins, broken down into 6 sections:

- **Vitamin A** — Role in night vision and immunity; deficiency consequences
- **Vitamin B** (B-complex) — Multiple B vitamins covering energy metabolism, nerve function, red blood cell production, and more. Historical context, a disease that crippled entire populations due to B1 (thiamine) deficiency
- **Vitamin C** — Antioxidant and immune support; famous for the history of _scurvy_ among sailors (hence the British nickname "limeys")
- **Vitamin D** — The "sunshine vitamin"; linked to bone health; deficiency caused _rickets_ in industrial-era cities
- **Vitamin E** — Antioxidant properties, sources include nuts and greens
- **Vitamin K** — Blood clotting and bone metabolism
